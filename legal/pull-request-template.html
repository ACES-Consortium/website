ACES contribution checklist

Change and affected sections

Describe the proposal, reasons, affected specification version/clauses and any technical behavior change.

Contributor and acceptance

Identify the Contributor's legal name, represented entity if any, and the applicable acceptance record.

  • For a consortium member: reference the existing membership acceptance. No duplicate agreement is required.
  • For a non-member: include or link the affirmative acceptance statement in CONTRIBUTOR-AGREEMENT.md.

Identify third-party sources, licenses and any relevant authority evidence reference. Do not publish confidential authorization documents or unnecessary personal data.

Submitter review

  • I am authorized to submit the identified material for the named Contributor under the applicable terms.
  • I have identified the applicable membership or non-member acceptance and Scope version.
  • I have identified third-party material and the separate license for code elements.
  • Any patent exclusion or withdrawal I am issuing follows Notices.md and the CSL requirements, rather than relying on this checklist alone.

Maintainer review

  • Confirm the existing member acceptance or non-member acceptance, represented party and contribution authority.
  • Review technical impact, Scope, provenance, attribution and code/specification license boundaries.
  • Record contribution dates and relevant notices/exclusion periods.
  • Preserve the distinction between a draft merge and Approved Specification designation or certification.

A question-only discussion by a non-member is not a completed contribution acceptance. These prompts support human review and do not install automated CLA checks or branch protection.
