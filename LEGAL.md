# ACES legal framework

**Version 1.0 | Effective 5 November 2026**

ACES is an open, vendor-neutral specification-development project. Its specification is available under the Community Specification License 1.0. Both commercial and open-source implementations are permitted under the applicable terms. Consortium membership is not required to read the specification, implement it or submit a technical proposal.

## Membership, contributions and governance

- [Membership terms](MEMBERSHIP.md): acceptance of the legal framework as a condition of membership.
- [Contributor agreement](CONTRIBUTOR-AGREEMENT.md): the agreement accepted through membership or an authorized non-member contribution.
- [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md): submission and review procedure.
- [Governance](Governance.md): official specification decisions, review and approval.
- [Identity and certification policy](TRADEMARKS.md): honest compatibility statements, official branding and certification claims.

Membership incorporates contributor acceptance. An implementer seeking the CSL Section 2.1 patent grants must also follow a Section 2.1.3 acceptance route. The full licenses govern; this index does not replace them or guarantee freedom from third-party rights. No product certification is conferred by membership or by implementing a draft.

Coordination and legal notices: **info@aces-consortium.org**. Notices for which CSL requires a repository pull request or commit must follow the procedure in `Notices.md`.
